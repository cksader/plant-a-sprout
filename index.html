ABOUT PLANT A SPROUT

A garden of
shared frequencies.

Inspired by the tiny sprouts we wear to raves: a little sign that we’re growing together. Draw something, give it a track, leave a memory.

This shared version stores drawings and memories in Supabase. Approved contributions are shared across devices and checked for updates every eight seconds. The 24 example sprouts have fictional memories and are not artist contributions. Earlier browser-only plantings are not automatically uploaded.

Music belongs to its creators. No recordings are included or played automatically. Optional garden tones are synthesized locally. Listening links need internet.
